# TRACK SEPARATION

This document prevents one data treatment, sample, or forecast origin from serving incompatible purposes.

| Issue | Structural inference | Forecasting | Policy reporting | Descriptive/context |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Human capital | Observed `emp × hc` baseline; latent/MI robustness only with uncertainty propagation | Predicted/imputed H allowed if origin-safe and OOS-validated | Posterior future H paths | Coverage and measurement comparison |
| TFP | Never ordinary RHS input; validates/measures latent productivity | Forecast signal/robustness | Productivity scenarios | Growth accounting |
| GDP missingness | No imputation | Forecast target remains observed only | No imputed policy outcome | Missingness maps allowed |
| Grouping | Initial-state development status + region | Origin-safe hierarchy | Current official status for policy labels | Income transitions |
| Small economies | ≥1M screen baseline; ≥5M sensitivity | Model-dependent | All policy-relevant countries with sufficient information | Report separately where useful |
| 2015 origin | Historical science | Sacred 2016–2019 holdout | Not current policy | — |
| 2019 origin | Historical methodology/stress | 2020–2023/25 stress and long-horizon historical-origin forecast | Not headline current policy | — |
| Current origin | Supplementary structural update | Current forecast | Headline 2028/29, 2030, 2043 policy exercise | — |
| Phillips–Sul | Not pooling validation | — | Convergence context only | Descriptive convergence clubs |
| Core-5 | Focal reporting/Nepal validation | Nepal-focused forecast comparison | South Asia policy context | Regional comparison |
| Post-2019 capital extensions | Not backfilled into historical inference as observed data | Allowed only in current-information track with labels | Allowed with provenance label | Compare against official ICSD endpoint |

## Admissibility rules

1. A treatment that improves forecasting is not automatically admissible for structural elasticity estimation.
2. Structural baseline estimates use observed headline inputs only.
3. Any generated regressor used in structural robustness must propagate its uncertainty.
4. Current-information policy probabilities must never be reported from the 2019-origin posterior.
5. Treaty/policy reporting universes are not automatically identical to structural estimation samples.
6. When rules conflict, the stricter track-specific rule governs.
